# Wave 5 — Motion Plan

## Principle

Nothing arrives merely to announce itself. Motion shows alignment, dependency, state, or controlled change.

## Intro

The final hero remains readable on first paint. Over no more than 1.1 seconds:

1. a short correction-blue caret marks the headline’s clause boundary;
2. the unchanged words resolve into their final line breaks and hierarchy;
3. navigation, support copy, and actions settle onto shared alignments;
4. the caret disappears, leaving blue only in its normal action/focus role.

Use transforms, opacity, and clipping. Never type the headline character by character, show a loader, block scrolling, or replay the sequence on every navigation. Session storage may suppress repeat playback. Reduced-motion users see the final composition immediately.

## Hero scroll

As the first viewport leaves, the two clauses separate slightly in depth and emphasis while the direct promise remains stable long enough to read. Movement stays within roughly 16–24px and is driven continuously by scroll progress.

## Systems chapter

One sticky proof field changes continuously beside three real records:

1. `3,500+ pages / WordPress → Prismic`
2. `MUI + Styled Components + shadcn/ui → Tailwind`
3. `change → compare → review / Playwright + GitHub Actions`

Scroll position controls which real relationship is emphasized and the progress of its alignment. Do not fabricate confidential UI. On mobile, show three static proof fields in source order with no pinning.

## Product sequences

MineCentral advances through `Create → Subscribe → Monitor`; Me Save Money advances through `Record → Budget → Remaining`. Scroll progress adjusts the active step and connector length. The sequence must still be clear with JavaScript disabled.

## Contact

The contact rule extends toward the email as the section enters. Hover or keyboard focus advances the arrow and changes the rule to correction blue.

## Limits

- no smooth-scroll hijacking or scroll trapping;
- no cursor replacement;
- no repeated opacity/translate reveal system;
- no count-up metrics;
- no parallax stacks or floating cards;
- no pinned section longer than roughly three viewports;
- preserve source order, keyboard access, and full content without JavaScript;
- reduced motion must remove movement without removing hierarchy or information.
